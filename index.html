Who we are

The Threshing Floor Ministries is a local church in the Riverside, CA area dedicated to sharing hope, faith, and restoration within our community. Our mission is to create spaces where people can gather, grow in faith, and experience genuine fellowship. “His winnowing fork is in His hand, and He will thoroughly clear His threshing floor…” - Matthew 3:12
